§01 — About

AI, code and building a company.

I’m a graduate engineering student at IMT Nord Europe (formerly Mines de Douai), one of France’s grandes écoles, after three years of integrated preparatory classes. I’m now focusing on artificial intelligence: I’ll spend January to May 2027 at ÉTS Montréal for a software engineering semester, I’m aiming for an MS in Machine Learning at Georgia Tech, and I’m looking for an AI internship in the United States.

What sets me apart is that I already know how to ship. I led ShakeUp from Figma mockups to the App Store with a team of five: a mobile app, an admin dashboard, about fifty serverless functions, and a first AI feature that turns a photo of a venue’s floor plan into structured data with an LLM.

On the business side, I’m turning ShakeUp into a SaaS so any event organizer in France can use it, and launching WikiStudent, a campus card game I built by orchestrating AI coding agents. My internship at CLEEVEN in Basel, pitching C-suite leaders at fast-growing startups and global enterprises in tech, banking and pharma, taught me the other half of the job: selling, negotiating and hiring.

Outside of work, I’ve been a national fencing referee since 2016 and I run the technical side of the Challenge Aramis in Lyon, an international tournament with 200+ fencers.
